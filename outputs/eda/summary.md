## What goes with the fee (price model, 2026 prices)
- **Market value:** Spearman ρ = 0.86 (n=1,555); the median fee is 1.00× the Transfermarkt value. 1 model purchases have no market value before the transfer.
- **Age:** ρ = -0.30; the median fee peaks at age 22 (€23.9m).
- **Origin club position:** ρ = -0.21 (a lower position number, a stronger club, goes with a higher fee). Medians: 1st–4th €22.0m, 5th–8th €18.4m, 9th–12th €13.3m, 13th–16th €14.1m, 17th–20th €9.4m.
- **Origin league** (median, n): Premier League €28.2m (315), Serie A €13.6m (378), Bundesliga €13.6m (270), Ligue 1 €13.4m (331), LaLiga €12.6m (262).
- **Contract:** Transfermarkt keeps only the current contract, so years left at the selling club are unknown for every summer. For 2026 the new contract length gives ρ = 0.41 with the fee (n=205); it is not a pre-transfer feature.

## Percentiles and profiles
Each purchase is ranked against bought players of the same position (ATT/MID/DEF), all summers pooled, with at least 900 league minutes the season before: reference sizes ATT 415, DEF 454, MID 384. It is not the whole league: Sofascore has no position for every player. Purchases under 900 minutes also get a percentile, flagged `low_minutes`. Possession lost, dribbled past and fouls are inverted (100 = best). Stored in `data/processed/percentiles.parquet`.

## Limits
- Correlations are bivariate and descriptive; market value already carries most of the information on performance.
- Percentiles compare bought players only, pooled over 2019–2026 (per 90 drift between seasons is ignored).
- Sofascore ball recoveries are missing for 60% of the model and are left out.
